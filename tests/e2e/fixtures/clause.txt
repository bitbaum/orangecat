Clause 11 — Non-compete. For a period of 24 months after the end of this employment, the Employee shall not, anywhere in Switzerland or the European Union, be employed by, advise, or hold any interest in any business that competes with the Employer's business of software development for the insurance industry. For each breach the Employee owes a penalty of CHF 50,000, without prejudice to the Employer's right to claim further damages and to demand that the Employee cease the activity. The Employee is a junior software developer with a gross annual salary of CHF 78,000 and no access to customer lists or pricing.
