### systemic#13 · low · At 320px the footer's last row is clipped on both sides

- **Where:** all 35 public pages with the footer (phone, both themes)
- **What a person meets:** On the smallest phones, and at 125% zoom on a 390px phone, the first and last footer links are cut off (WCAG 1.4.10 Reflow).
- **Cause:** src/components/layout/Footer.tsx:148: `flex items-center space-x-6 text-sm` puts four links in one row that cannot wrap; the overflow is hidden by overflow-x: clip at src/app/globals.css:22-23 and :38-39.
- **Fix:** Footer.tsx:148 `flex items-center space-x-6 text-sm` -> `grid grid-cols-2 justify-items-center gap-x-6 text-sm sm:flex sm:items-center`. An even 2x2 grid holds at every phone width; from sm it is one row as today. No change needed in footerLinkClass. The viewport-box assertion proposed for check:broken-words is still worth adding, since overflow-x: clip hides this from the document-width check.
- **Mind:** Plain flex-wrap produces the 3 + 1 orphan row that fleet 'One shape per kind' rules out; an even grid is the named alternative.
