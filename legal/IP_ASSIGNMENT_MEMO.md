## 0. What changed, and why this document was rewritten

The 2026-06-02 version of this file was a *pre-incorporation IP assignment memorandum*: it
declared an intent to assign all work product to a planned Swiss holding company ("bitbaum AG",
Canton of Zug) and product subsidiaries ("orangecat GmbH", "Loki GmbH").

**None of those entities were ever incorporated, and there is no active plan to incorporate them.**

The original also recorded three things that are no longer true: it named the repositories as
`github.com/g-but/cockpit` and `github.com/g-but/orangecat` — a personal account that has since
been retired, and a project name ("Cockpit") that is now **Loki** — and it described both
repositories as private when both are public.

Rather than maintain a paper trail of intent toward a structure that does not exist, this file now
records only what is actually true: who wrote the work, who holds the copyright, and under what
licence it is published.
